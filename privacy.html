Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

  • Notched Music does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Everything you create in the app stays on your iPhone.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Who we are

Notched Music (“Notched” or “the app”) is published by David Anderson (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information. If you have questions, contact us at danderson.hermes+notchedmusic@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Notched has no servers and makes no network connections to us or to any third party. We never receive the information you enter or create in the app.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage:

DataPurpose
Tinnitus frequency profile (matched pitch, ear, relative loudness, test results)Set the frequency removed from your music
Music files you import, and the processed (“notched”) copiesPlay your therapy audio
Song details (title, artist, album, artwork)Show your library
Listening session times and durationsShow your listening progress

This data never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple under your Apple Account settings and Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Purchases

Notched Music is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes your payment and handles your purchase information. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. Apple may share aggregated, non-identifying sales and usage statistics with us through App Store Connect, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Third parties, analytics, and advertising

  • The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, crash-reporting services, or advertising.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

Deleting your data

You are always in control:

  • Profiles: open Settings → Delete all profiles in the app.
  • Everything: delete the app from your iPhone. This permanently removes all of its data from the device.

Children

Notched is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health information and medical disclaimer

Your tinnitus profile may be considered health-related information. It is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Notched Music is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. It is not a substitute for professional advice. Consult an audiologist or physician about tinnitus or hearing concerns.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data a company holds about you. Because we do not collect or hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. Your on-device data can be deleted at any time as described above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, for example by adding optional sync or an account, we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the App Store release notes.
